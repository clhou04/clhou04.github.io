Current Project

Could we Detect HNC with High Resolution Spectroscopy with ELT

High resolution spectroscopy has proven successful in detecting major atmospheric biomarkers such as CO, H20, and CH4 in hot Jupiters and other gas giants. With ELT going online, we may obtain the necessary resolution to observe the spectral emission from Earth-like terrestrial planets. A major part of this endeaver is seeing around our own atmosphere, which proves possible with the radial velocity of the orbiting planet, allowing us to see past the telluric absorption lines. To study this possibility, my project involves simulating mock ELT ANDES high-resolution observations of Earth-like planets in forward models, incorperating realistic noise, stellar activity, and telluric correction. This project will test how well ELT is capable of detecting atmospheric constituents, including potential biosignatures and prebiosignatures.
